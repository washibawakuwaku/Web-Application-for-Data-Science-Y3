## 1. Business Understanding

- **Problem:** Recording attendance on paper / spreadsheets is slow and error-prone, and
  gives teachers no easy way to see who is falling behind.
- **Users:** Teachers, school administrators, and students.
- **Goal:** Store attendance reliably and turn it into clear analytics — rates, trends
  and low-attendance alerts — so staff can act early.
- **Success criteria:** every attendance session is one click to record; the dashboard
  shows the current rate; students below **75%** are flagged automatically.

## 2. Data Understanding  *(notebook `01_attendance_eda.ipynb`)*

- **Source:** the system's own database, exported with `analysis/export_data.py`
  (see `data/README.md`).
- **Size:** ≈ 984 rows × 16 columns; one row per (student, course, day).
- **Data quality problems found & handled:** invalid status values, unparseable dates,
  and duplicate records for the same student/course/day (kept the latest).

## 3. Data Preparation  *(`analysis/make_dataset.py`)*

| Problem | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Rows with an unknown status | drop | only present/late/absent are meaningful |
| Unparseable `date` | drop | a record with no date can't be placed in time |
| Duplicate (student, course, day) | keep the last | one attendance record per session |

- **Features created:** `weekday`, `week_start`, `month`, `is_present/late/absent`,
  `attended` — used for the trend and weekday analyses.
- **Consistency:** the analytics functions are shared with the live API
  (`backend_django/api/stats.py` ↔ `analysis/metrics.py`), so the report and the app
  always show the same numbers.

## 4. Attendance Analytics  *(features 6, 8, 9)*

- **Attendance rate** = `(present + late) / total × 100`.
- **Dashboard (feature 6):** overall rate, present/late/absent counts, rate by class and
  by course, rate over time.
- **Low-attendance alerts (feature 8):** students below the 75% threshold; *critical*
  when below 80% of the threshold (60%).
- **Trend analysis (feature 9):** rate per week/month with change vs. the previous
  period, absence by weekday, most-frequently-absent students, and sharp drops.
